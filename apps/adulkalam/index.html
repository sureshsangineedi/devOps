Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India | Former President of India

About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a key role in India's missile and nuclear programs.

Early Life

He was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He came from a humble background and worked hard to achieve his dreams.

Achievements

  • Known as the "Missile Man of India"
  • Worked with ISRO and DRDO
  • Key role in Pokhran-II nuclear tests
  • Received Bharat Ratna (India's highest civilian award)

Presidency

He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007 and was loved by people, especially students.

Famous Quote

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
